Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · October 2026 · Last updated 2026-11-10

Your health records and camera data stay on this phone. Apple or Google and RevenueCat process purchase information for Premium.

What Jaw Coach keeps, and where

To run your program, Jaw Coach saves on this phone: your profile (goals and birth year), safety-screen outcomes (never your individual answers), pain and feel logs, sessions and sets, Jaw Check results, your level and streak, and your settings.

Health information is kept in a private folder inside the app that is excluded from iCloud and device backups. Settings and purchase status live in the app's standard preferences.

The camera

Camera frames are processed in memory, on this phone, to find face landmarks and count reps. Frames are never saved, recorded or sent anywhere. Only the numbers a set produces, such as rep timing, are kept.

What we collect

Jaw Coach has no account, no analytics of your health or exercise activity, no advertising and no attribution SDKs. Nothing about your jaw, your pain or your face leaves this phone unless you share it yourself. Purchase processing is described below.

Purchases

If you buy Premium, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google, and by RevenueCat, which manages subscriptions for us. They receive purchase information only, such as the product, price, date and an anonymous app user ID. This is used to validate purchases, manage Premium access and provide purchase reports. They never receive jaw, pain, face, level or program data.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on this phone. Jaw Coach asks for permission only when you turn a reminder on.

Sharing, export and deletion

  • Export: Settings › Your data › Export my data makes a file you can save or send. You choose where it goes.
  • The summary for a dentist or physio is shared only when you share it.
  • Delete everything: Settings › Your data removes all health data from this phone straight away. Deleting the app does the same.

Consumer health data notice

Some laws, such as Washington's My Health My Data Act, give you rights over consumer health data. Jaw Coach's health data stays on your device and is not collected, shared or sold by Inkstroke, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You can see (export) and delete your data yourself at any time in Settings.

Age

Jaw Coach is for adults aged 18 and over. Setup stops if you are under 18.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app with a new date. Questions: nanket.dev@gmail.com

This website and support email

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and has no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies or account forms. GitHub processes connection information, such as IP addresses, to serve and secure the pages. If you email support, your email provider and ours process the message, and we receive the information you choose to send. Please do not send health records or camera recordings.
