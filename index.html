The exercises

Short daily doses adapted from guidance written for adults. More isn't better for your joints.

Slow CloseOpen within comfort, then close slowly and smoothly.
Side GlideGlide your jaw side to side: slow, mid-range, never forced.
Forward GlideEase your lower jaw forward and back, slowly.
Open HoldHold a steady, comfortable opening, not your maximum.
Chin TuckGlide your head straight back, eyes level, then relax.
Rest ResetLips together, teeth apart, slow breathing through your nose.
Open, Forward and Side PressLight resistance in each direction. Nothing moves.
Rhythm ChewAn easy, even chewing rhythm. Steadiness, not speed.
O–E VowelsAlternate round and wide lip shapes to wake up your face.

Questions

How does Jaw Coach count reps?

It uses your iPhone's front camera to follow your jaw and counts a rep only when the movement is real. It also checks tempo and steadiness. The camera frames are processed on your phone and are never saved or sent anywhere.

Will jaw exercises change how my jawline looks?

Jaw Coach doesn't promise that. It trains what can actually be trained: slow, controlled movement, a comfortable range, steadiness and a daily habit, with an honest record of what you did.

Is Jaw Coach a mewing app?

No. Jaw Coach coaches guided jaw movement exercises. The American Association of Orthodontists says there is no evidence for mewing. Read the AAO article.

Is it safe?

There's a short safety check before you start, a Hurts button in every exercise, and a gentle Comfort track for sensitive jaws. Pain changes the plan to lighter days or rest. Jaw Coach is not a medical device; if you have a jaw problem, ask a professional first.
