Jaw Coach support

Email nanket.dev@gmail.com. For purchase help, include your app version and whether you use iPhone or Android. Please don't email camera recordings, health logs, passwords or payment details.

Restore Premium

Open Settings → Premium → Restore purchases, signed in with the store account that made the purchase.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Use Manage subscription in the app, or your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (or the free trial) to avoid the next charge.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Camera and counting tips

  • Prop your phone at eye level, about an arm's length away.
  • Keep your face evenly lit, without a bright window behind you.
  • Each move starts by learning your relaxed jaw and a comfortable range, so counting fits you.

Your data

Use Settings → Your data to export or delete your on-device data. Inkstroke can't access your health records; they stay on your phone. See the privacy policy.

Health concerns

Jaw Coach is a wellness app, not a medical device. Stop if anything hurts, catches or locks, and tap Hurts. For health concerns, contact a qualified health professional.
